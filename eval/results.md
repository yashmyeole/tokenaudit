# Detector evaluation

Every detector, scored against all 30 labeled corpus fixtures (including the negative fixtures where a correct tool must stay silent, and variants with message content deliberately missing). Generated by `python eval/run_eval.py` — edit code or corpus, never this file.

Read the numbers for what they are: **correctness counts against a corpus with known answers — a floor, not field precision.** The corpus is small on purpose (every case is hand-labeled and byte-reproducible), and the simulator shares assumptions with the detectors, so real-world evidence comes from the captured-runs case studies, not this table. An abstention on a fixture without captured content is correct behavior, reported in its own column — refusing to guess is part of the contract.
